# Project Brief Template (non-software)

For campaigns, reels/videos, events, client deliverables, processes. Adapt section names to the work; keep the order.

```
# <Project Name> — Project Brief
Version · Date · Owner · Status

## 1. Objective
What success looks like, in 2–3 lines. Primary goal and secondary goals.

## 2. Audience
Who it's for: segment, location, language (Tamil / English / bilingual), what they care about.

## 3. Key Message & Tone
Core message, supporting points, tone/voice, call to action.

## 4. Deliverables
Table: Deliverable | Format / specs | Quantity | Owner | Due
(e.g. 30s vertical reel 1080×1920, 3 static posts, WhatsApp copy, landing page)

## 5. Channels & Distribution
Where it goes live (Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp, SMS, print, portals), posting plan.

## 6. Content Outline
Per deliverable: structure, script or storyboard beats, visuals, captions.

## 7. Brand & Creative Direction
Look and feel, references to avoid copying, logos/credits required.

## 8. Compliance
Only what applies: SMS DLT templates, ad platform policies, ASCI/claims, consent for people shown, RERA numbers for property ads.

## 9. Budget & Pricing
Spend by line item; if it's a client job, the fee and payment terms.

## 10. Timeline
Table: Milestone | Date | Owner

## 11. Success Metrics
Measurable targets (reach, leads, conversions, enquiries, CPL).

## 12. Assumptions
Every default chosen on the user's behalf, marked "(assumed)".

## 13. Open Questions & Risks

## Changelog
```
